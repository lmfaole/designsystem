# Forms

Forms are rare in a read-mostly app, and so they are small: one column, a few fields, native controls. The rules are from GOV.UK
(validation, error summary, date input), Carbon (forms pattern), Atlassian (form) and Primer (toggle switch), put into our terms.

## 1. One column, label above

<TextField label="E-postadresse" name="epost" hint="For eksempel ola@eksempel.no." />

- The label is above the field and always visible. A placeholder is never the label.
- The hint says the format and stays visible; so does the error. There is no tooltip.
- Mark only the minority: «(valgfritt)» when most fields are required.
- Read-only is shown as text in a `FactList`. Avoid `disabled`: say why the value cannot be changed instead.

## 2. Validate when the form is sent

On submit, not while typing (GOV.UK). Carbon validates when the reader leaves a field; we follow GOV.UK because it keeps one rule for the whole form. Validate on the server as well. Do not use the HTML `required` attribute: use `novalidate` and say what is
needed in words.

When there are errors:

1. Show a `FormErrorSummary` at the top of the page and move focus to it.
2. List every error as a link to its field, in the same words as beside the field.
3. Keep what the reader wrote in the fields.
4. Start the page title with «Feil:».

<FormErrorSummary errors={[{ field: "epost", message: "Skriv en e-postadresse med @." }]} />

## 3. Words

Say what is wrong and how to fix it: «Skriv en e-postadresse med @, for eksempel ola@eksempel.no.» Never «Vennligst», «Beklager», «Ups»,
«ugyldig» or «du glemte». An error that is not about a field (no access, the service is down) is not a field error: it is a `Banner` or
an `ErrorMessage` for the part that failed.

## 4. Search and choice

<SearchField label="Søk i pull requests" action="#" />

A search is a GET form that goes to the same page with `?q=`, so the result has an address. It stands above the list with its label
visible, not behind an icon. Few known values are `FilterChips`; a known list is a `NativeSelect`. A calendar popover and an
autocomplete draw a menu in the page and are not part of the system; a date is a native date field, or three fields (day, month, year) when the reader knows it by heart.

## 5. Saving

A switch takes effect at once and the label names the setting, not the state. A checkbox needs an explicit save. Use one save pattern in a form.
The button names the verb («Lagre», «Opprett»). After saving, say it in the page («Lagret», a `Banner` or a line next to the form),
never in a toast, and take the reader to what they made.
