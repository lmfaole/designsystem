# Colour and state

The interface is monochrome. There is **one** colour in it, `destructive`, for what stops something and is for you to fix. Everything else is told in form: a symbol and words. Six `series-*` hues exist for a chart that is given colours of its own; they carry no meaning and are never a state. The values, their pairs and their measured contrast are in **Tokens / Overview**.

## 1. Roles

| Token | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `background`, `foreground` | The page and its text |
| `muted`, `muted-foreground` | Quiet surfaces and secondary text |
| `primary`, `primary-foreground` | The one strong action |
| `secondary`, `accent` | Hover and selected surfaces |
| `destructive` | What stops something |
| `border`, `input`, `ring` | Lines, field edges, the focus ring |
| `chart-1` to `chart-5` | A grey ramp for chart marks (see Data visualisation and Tokens / Overview) |
| `series-1` to `series-6` | Six hues for a chart given colours of its own; never a state |

A page uses the tokens and never a colour of its own. Light and dark are the same tokens with other values.

## 2. State is never colour alone

A state has **a symbol, a word and a place**: <Badge icon="failure" tone="destructive">Feilet</Badge> <Badge icon="pull-request-draft">Utkast</Badge>
<Badge tone="muted">Arkivert</Badge>. Use one word for one meaning across the page, and put the symbol before the word. Attention goes from
what needs the reader now (`destructive`), through feedback and progress, to what only changed.

## 3. Contrast

Text is 4.5:1 against its background, and 3:1 for large text and for the parts of controls and marks that carry meaning (Atlassian, WCAG). The
table in **Tokens / Overview** has the measured ratio of every pair, in light and dark. Secondary text (`muted-foreground`) is 6.0:1 on the page and 5.5:1 on a muted
surface in light. `border` is 1.3:1 in light, so a line between parts is never the only edge of something the reader must find.

## 4. Selected and focused

A selected item and a focused item are different things. When both apply, both show (Atlassian): the focus ring is never removed from the selected one.
