# Tokens

The tokens are the custom properties of `src/theme.css` (colour, radius, the fonts) and `src/motion.css` (durations and easings), in a light
and a dark set. A page uses them through the components and never writes a colour, a radius or a duration of its own. This page says what each
is for and how it measures; the live swatches are in the other pages of **Tokens**.

The contrast ratios are **calculated from the values in `theme.css`** (WCAG relative luminance, the colour converted from oklch; a value with transparency is laid over the surface
behind it, mixed in encoded sRGB the way a browser does). `src/theme-contrast.test.ts` works them out on every `npm test` and fails when a text pair is under 4.5:1 or a mark that carries meaning is under 3:1, so the numbers below are checked, not just written.

## 1. Colour roles

The interface is monochrome: the one colour in it is `destructive`, and what a state means is told with a symbol and words, never with colour
alone. The six `series-*` hues exist for a chart that asks for colours of its own and carry no meaning.

| Token | For | Light on page | Dark on page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `background`, `foreground` | The page and its text | 19.8 | 19.0 |
| `card`, `card-foreground` | A card and its text (a card is one self-contained item) | 19.8 | 17.2 |
| `popover`, `popover-foreground` | A dialog or popover and its text | same as card | same as card |
| `primary`, `primary-foreground` | The one strong action, and text on it | 17.2 | 14.2 |
| `secondary`, `secondary-foreground` | A quiet surface and text on it | 16.4 | 14.5 |
| `accent`, `accent-foreground` | A hovered or selected surface and text on it | 16.4 | 14.5 |
| `muted` | A quiet surface (a code box, a skeleton) | — | — |
| `muted-foreground` | Secondary text | 6.0 | 7.6 |
| `destructive` | What stops something and is for you to fix | 5.6 | 6.8 |
| `border` | Lines between parts | 1.3 | 1.3 |
| `input` | A decorative line at the edge of a field | 1.3 | 1.5 |
| `ring` | The focus ring | 4.7 | 4.2 |
| `logo-plate` | The light plate under a dark logo | — | — |

How to read the numbers: text needs **4.5:1**, and the parts of a control or a mark that carry meaning need **3:1** (WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11).

- **Secondary text** (`muted-foreground`) is 6.0:1 on the page and 5.5:1 on a `muted` surface in light, and 7.6:1 and 5.8:1 in dark, so it can be used for
  small text on both. (It was 4.3:1 on a `muted` surface in light until 8.8, which is why the value was darkened.)
- **`border` and `input` are for a line between parts, not for the edge of something the reader must find.** They are 1.3:1 in light and 1.3 and 1.5:1 in dark (they are
  translucent, so they also change with what is behind them), so they never mark a control on its own: a field takes its edge from `muted-foreground` (`TextField`, `SearchField`) and a button has a fill or a visible edge of its own.
- **Check a pair on `card` as well as on the page.** `muted-foreground` is 6.0:1 on both in light and 7.6 and 6.9:1 in dark; `destructive` is 5.6:1 on both in light and 6.8 and 6.2:1 in dark, 5.2:1 on `muted`, and 4.7:1 on its own 10 % tint (the destructive button and badge), which the test checks since the stories' axe run found it at 4.0:1.
- **The focus ring** is 4.7:1 on the page in light and 4.2:1 in dark, so it can always be seen. It is never removed.
- Pair a surface only with its own `-foreground`: `primary` with `primary-foreground`, never with `foreground`.

## 2. Charts

| Token | Value | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default series (`StackedColumnChart`) | `foreground` at 100, 66 and 44 %, then `series-4` and on | The series of a chart that is not given colours, first series strongest |
| Default lines (`LineChart`) | `foreground` at 100, 72, 52 and 44 %, told apart by dash | Up to four lines |
| `chart-1` to `chart-5` | A grey ramp, the same in light and dark | The grey steps for marks that are not given a colour |
| `series-1` to `series-6` | Six hues, one lightness: 0.5 in light and 0.78 in dark | A chart a page gives colours of its own (`colors`, `DonutChart` `color`) |
| `temperature-warm`, `temperature-cold`, `precipitation` | A red-orange, a blue and a sea blue; 3:1 on the page and the card in both themes (a test) | The `Meteogram`'s temperature above and below 0° and its precipitation (`--weather-rain` wins on a `WeatherStage`) |
| `transit-bus`, `transit-tram`, `transit-metro`, `transit-rail`, `transit-ferry`, `transit-other`, each with its `-foreground`; `transit-late` | Ruter's red, blue, orange, a green, a teal and a grey; pale with dark text in dark. Text on each 4.5:1, each 3:1 on the page and the card, `transit-late` 4.5:1 (a test) | A line's kind in a `LineBadge` and the dots of a `DepartureBoard`'s strips; the delay of a departure («+3 min») |

What this means for a chart, measured on the page background:

- The default steps keep **3:1 on the page and on the card in light and dark**: 100, 66 and 44 % of the foreground are 19.8, 6.6 and 3.1:1 in light (19.0, 8.4 and 4.2 in dark), and the line steps 100, 72, 52 and 44 % are as strong. A test checks it. **The steps that used to be the defaults, 36 and 24 %, are
  2.4 and 1.7:1 in light** (3.2 and 2.0 in dark) and are not used: a mark told apart only by a step that light is not enough. (Until 8.13 this page gave 2.7 and 1.9, which was worked out with the colours blended in linear light; a browser blends in encoded sRGB, as `theme-contrast.test.ts` does.) A chart still names its series on the chart and keeps the gap between parts (guide «Data visualisation»).
- `chart-1` is 1.5:1 on a light page and `chart-3` to `chart-5` are 2.5, 1.9 and 1.3:1 on a dark one, because the ramp is the same in both modes. Do not
  rely on a `chart-*` step alone for a mark that carries meaning.
- The `series-*` colours are six that differ in **lightness as well as hue**, so they can be told apart without colour vision. They are 3.3 to 12.0:1 on a light page and 4.9 to 15.6:1 on a dark one (4.5 to 14.1 on a dark card),
  and they sit inside the sRGB gamut, so a browser shows them as written. Measured with the simulations of Machado et al. (2009), no two of them are closer than 16 in oklab distance (times 100; 8 is plainly different) in normal vision, protanopia, deuteranopia or
  tritanopia. Before 8.11 they were six hues of one lightness, and two pairs were 1.2 and 0.6 apart for deuteranopia. They carry no meaning and a chart that uses them still names its series, because colour is never the only signal. They are never used for a state.

## 3. Radius

`--radius` is 0.625 rem. The steps are multiples of it: `sm` 0.6, `md` 0.8, `lg` 1, `xl` 1.4, `2xl` 1.8, `3xl` 2.2 and `4xl` 2.6. A component picks its
step; a page does not. Things of one kind have one radius, and a nested box has a smaller radius than the one around it.

## 4. Fonts

One superfamily, **Atkinson Hyperlegible**, drawn by the Braille Institute so that letters that look alike are told apart (I, l and 1;
O and 0). **Next** (`font-sans`, and `font-heading`, which is the same) is all text and every heading; **Mono** (`font-mono`) is short
uppercase labels (`Heading look="label"`), code and technical values. The two share their x-height, capitals and figures, so they sit on
one line together. Both are shipped with the package (Fontsource), so a page works without the network. Figures that line up (a column, a counter) use tabular numbers (`Text numeric`).

## 5. Motion

Durations and easings are tokens too: see the guide **Motion**. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every duration is 0.01 ms.

## 6. Adding or changing a token

1. A new colour is a new **role** in the table above, with its pair, its two values and the contrast measured; never a literal in a component.
2. Change a value in `theme.css` only with the contrast in view: the pairs in the table must stay at 4.5:1 (text) and 3:1 (marks and edges).
3. Add the swatch to the story of its group.
