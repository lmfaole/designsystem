# designsystem

The design system of lmfaole's apps: the **tokens** (colours, radius, fonts, motion), the **building blocks** a page is put
together from, and the **components** built on them. React 19 and Tailwind 4, shipped as source.

- **Tokens** shows every colour, series colour, radius, font and motion token, live from `theme.css`.
- **Building blocks** is layout and text: `Stack`, `Inline`, `Grid`, `Split`, `Text`, `Heading`.
- **Components** is everything else, one page per component with its props.
- **Charts** are the TanStack Charts components, themed through `chart-frame`.
- **Pages** are the frame and the parts of a whole page.

The colour scheme follows your system (`prefers-color-scheme`): switch your OS to dark to see the dark tokens.
Every story is also checked by the **Accessibility** panel at the bottom.

The rules a project follows (no styling of its own, closed props, tokens only, a select is a native `<select>`)
are in the repository's README.
