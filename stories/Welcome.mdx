# designsystem

The design system of lmfaole's apps: the **tokens** (colours, radius, fonts, motion), the **building blocks** a page is put
together from, and the **components** built on them. React 19 and Tailwind 4, shipped as source.

- **Tokens**: every colour, series colour, radius, font and motion token, one story each, read live from `theme.css`.
- **Layout** and **Typography**: `Stack`, `Inline`, `Grid`, `Split`, the page frame, `Text`, `Heading` and links.
- **Primitives**: the generated shadcn/ui parts (button, card, native select, table …).
- **Navigation**, **Lists**, **Media**, **Tables**, **Charts**: the components a page shows its data with.
- **Feedback**, **Overlays**, **Motion**, **CV**: errors, dialogs and sheets, entrances, and the CV page.

Every component has its own page with its props (change them under Controls) and its variants as stories.

The colour scheme follows your system (`prefers-color-scheme`): switch your OS to dark to see the dark tokens.
Every story is also checked by the **Accessibility** panel at the bottom.

The rules a project follows (no styling of its own, closed props, tokens only, a select is a native `<select>`)
are in the repository's README.
