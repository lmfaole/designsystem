# Tokens

The tokens are the custom properties of `src/theme.css` (colour, radius, the fonts) and `src/motion.css` (durations and easings), in a light
and a dark set. A page uses them through the components and never writes a colour, a radius or a duration of its own. This page says what each
is for and how it measures; the live swatches are in the other pages of **Tokens**.

The contrast ratios are **calculated from the values in `theme.css`** (WCAG relative luminance, the colour converted from oklch; a value with
transparency is laid over the page background). They are as exact as that conversion; check a new colour with a contrast tool before you rely on it.

## 1. Colour roles

The interface is monochrome: the one colour in it is `destructive`, and what a state means is told with a symbol and words, never with colour
alone. The six `series-*` hues exist for a chart that asks for colours of its own and carry no meaning.

| Token | For | Light on page | Dark on page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `background`, `foreground` | The page and its text | 19.8 | 19.0 |
| `card`, `card-foreground` | A card and its text (a card is one self-contained item) | 19.8 | 17.2 |
| `popover`, `popover-foreground` | A dialog or popover and its text | same as card | same as card |
| `primary`, `primary-foreground` | The one strong action, and text on it | 17.2 | 14.2 |
| `secondary`, `secondary-foreground` | A quiet surface and text on it | 16.4 | 14.5 |
| `accent`, `accent-foreground` | A hovered or selected surface and text on it | 16.4 | 14.5 |
| `muted` | A quiet surface (a code box, a skeleton) | — | — |
| `muted-foreground` | Secondary text | 6.0 | 7.6 |
| `destructive` | What stops something and is for you to fix | 4.8 | 6.8 |
| `border` | Lines between parts | 1.3 | 2.9 |
| `input` | The edge of a field | 1.3 | 3.8 |
| `ring` | The focus ring | 4.7 | 4.2 |
| `logo-plate` | The light plate under a dark logo | — | — |

How to read the numbers: text needs **4.5:1**, and the parts of a control or a mark that carry meaning need **3:1** (WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11).

- **Secondary text** (`muted-foreground`) is 6.0:1 on the page and 5.5:1 on a `muted` surface in light, and 7.6:1 and 5.8:1 in dark, so it can be used for
  small text on both. (It was 4.3:1 on a `muted` surface in light until 8.8, which is why the value was darkened.)
- **`border` is for a line between parts, not for the edge of something the reader must find.** In light it is 1.3:1, so it never marks a control on its own:
  a field takes its edge from `muted-foreground` (`TextField`, `SearchField`) and a button has a fill or a visible edge of its own.
- **The focus ring** is 4.7:1 on the page in light and 4.2:1 in dark, so it can always be seen. It is never removed.
- Pair a surface only with its own `-foreground`: `primary` with `primary-foreground`, never with `foreground`.

## 2. Charts

| Token | Value | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default series (`StackedColumnChart`) | `foreground` at 100, 72, 52, 36 and 24 % | The series of a chart that is not given colours, first series strongest |
| `chart-1` to `chart-5` | A grey ramp, the same in light and dark | The grey steps for marks that are not given a colour |
| `series-1` to `series-6` | Six hues, one lightness: 0.5 in light and 0.78 in dark | A chart a page gives colours of its own (`colors`, `DonutChart` `color`) |

What this means for a chart, measured on the page background:

- The default series steps are 19.8, 9.8 and 4.7:1 at 100, 72 and 52 % in light (19.0, 8.9 and 4.7 in dark). **The two lightest steps (36 and 24 %) are
  about 2.7 and 1.9:1**, below 3:1 in both modes. A mark told apart only by those steps is not enough: name the series on the chart and keep the gap
  between parts (guide «Data visualisation»).
- `chart-1` is 1.5:1 on a light page and `chart-3` to `chart-5` are 2.5, 1.9 and 1.3:1 on a dark one, because the ramp is the same in both modes. Do not
  rely on a `chart-*` step alone for a mark that carries meaning.
- The `series-*` hues are 5.2 to 6.5:1 in light and 9.4 to 10.4:1 in dark: they meet 3:1 on the page. They are told apart by hue, so a chart that uses them
  still names its series (colour is never the only signal), and they are never used for a state.

## 3. Radius

`--radius` is 0.625 rem. The steps are multiples of it: `sm` 0.6, `md` 0.8, `lg` 1, `xl` 1.4, `2xl` 1.8, `3xl` 2.2 and `4xl` 2.6. A component picks its
step; a page does not. Things of one kind have one radius, and a nested box has a smaller radius than the one around it.

## 4. Fonts

**Geist** is the body and the interface; **JetBrains Mono** is the headings (`h1` to `h6` get `font-heading`), identifiers and code. Both are shipped with the package
(Fontsource), so a page works without the network. Figures that line up (a column, a counter) use tabular numbers (`Text numeric`).

## 5. Motion

Durations and easings are tokens too: see the guide **Motion**. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every duration is 0.01 ms.

## 6. Adding or changing a token

1. A new colour is a new **role** in the table above, with its pair, its two values and the contrast measured; never a literal in a component.
2. Change a value in `theme.css` only with the contrast in view: the pairs in the table must stay at 4.5:1 (text) and 3:1 (marks and edges).
3. Add the swatch to the story of its group.
