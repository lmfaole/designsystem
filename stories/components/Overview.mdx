# Components

Grouped by what they are for, in the order a page is built: its frame, its words and links, what a person does, then the kinds of
content. Find what you need on the left and follow the name. Whole pages made of these are under **Patterns**; tokens and icons under
**Foundations**.

If the thing is not here, add a component to the design system; do not style it in the app.

## Layout

The frame of a page and how its parts stand: one reading column, sections under each other, columns only for what is looked at.

| You need | Use |
| --- | --- |
| The page frame and its title | [`PageLayout`](?path=/docs/components-layout-pagelayout--docs), [`PageColumn`](?path=/docs/components-layout-pagecolumn--docs), [`PageHeader`](?path=/docs/components-layout-pageheader--docs) |
| The head of a page of one thing: its picture and name together, on a backdrop of the picture's colours | [`DetailHeader`](?path=/docs/components-layout-detailheader--docs) |
| The weather of a page (now, today) on a backdrop of the weather's own colours | [`WeatherStage`](?path=/docs/components-layout-weatherstage--docs) |
| A titled part of a page; parts that belong together | [`PageSection`](?path=/docs/components-layout-pagesection--docs), [`SectionGroup`](?path=/docs/components-layout-sectiongroup--docs) |
| Things under each other; short things in a line | [`Stack`](?path=/docs/components-layout-stack--docs), [`Inline`](?path=/docs/components-layout-inline--docs) |
| Things looked at in columns; a pane beside the main content | [`Grid`](?path=/docs/components-layout-grid--docs), [`Split`](?path=/docs/components-layout-split--docs) |
| A box around one thing; a line between | [`Card`](?path=/docs/components-layout-card--docs), [`Separator`](?path=/docs/components-layout-separator--docs) |

## Onboarding

The first visit: the introduction, whose data, what a page is for, a permission, what is new, and the offer to install; and the settings that change it later. What has been seen is kept in the `seen` cookie (`lib/onboarding`), what the visitor chose in the `prefs` cookie (`lib/preferences`), both read on the server.

| You need | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Whose data to fetch; whose is shown | [`UsernameForm`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-usernameform--docs), [`UserChip`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-userchip--docs) |
| A word about a page on the first visit | [`IntroCallout`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-introcallout--docs) |
| A permission of the browser (position, notifications) | [`PermissionCard`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-permissioncard--docs) |
| A place or section that has just come | [`NewBadge`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-newbadge--docs) |
| The offer to install the app | [`InstallPrompt`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-installprompt--docs) |
| The first visit's introduction, a step at a time | [`FirstRun`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-firstrun--docs) (with `FirstRunEnhancer`) |
| The settings page, a part at a time | [`SettingsSection`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-settingssection--docs) |
| What a choice of theme, effects or tilt gives | [`ThemeSample`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-themesample--docs), [`EffectsPreview`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-effectspreview--docs), [`TiltPreview`](?path=/docs/components-onboarding-tiltpreview--docs) |
